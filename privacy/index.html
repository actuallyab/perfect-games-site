Perfect Games Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to all mobile applications developed and published by Perfect Games, including but not limited to:

  • Perfect Drop
  • Perfect Chase

and any future mobile applications published under the Perfect Games brand (collectively, the “Apps”). By downloading, installing, or using any App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

We do not require account creation to play our Apps, and we do not collect a personal profile from you. The information collected falls into the following categories:

1.1 Anonymous Gameplay Analytics

We collect anonymous, aggregated data about how the Apps are used — for example, levels played, session length, in-app actions, and feature engagement — to understand and improve the player experience.

1.2 Crash Reports

If an App crashes or encounters an error, diagnostic information (such as stack traces and the state of the app at the time of the crash) is automatically collected to help us identify and fix bugs.

1.3 Device Information

We may collect technical information about your device, including but not limited to:

  • Device model and manufacturer
  • App version
  • Android or iOS operating system version
  • Language and region settings
  • Advertising identifiers (e.g. Google Advertising ID / Apple IDFA), where applicable and permitted by your device settings

1.4 Data Collected via Third-Party Services

Our Apps may use the following third-party services, each of which may collect information as described in their own privacy policies:

  • Firebase Analytics — anonymous usage and engagement analytics
  • Firebase Crashlytics — crash and error reporting
  • Firebase Remote Config — remote delivery of app configuration values
  • Google AdMob — advertising, where an App displays ads
  • Google Play Billing — processing of in-app purchases and subscriptions
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (Push Notifications) — delivery of optional push notifications, where enabled
Currently: no account creation is required to play any Perfect Games app; no personal profile is collected; we do not collect contacts, photos, or location data; and our Apps do not use microphone or camera access.

2. How We Use Information

Information collected is used to:

  • Improve gameplay, features, and overall app quality
  • Improve player retention and understand engagement
  • Measure and analyze usage through analytics
  • Identify, diagnose, and fix crashes and bugs
  • Deliver app updates and remotely configured content
  • Serve advertisements, where applicable
  • Process in-app purchases and subscriptions

3. Third-Party Services

Our Apps rely on the following third-party providers to operate core functionality. Each provider processes data under its own privacy policy and terms:

  • Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config, Cloud Messaging) — firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google AdMob — policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • Google Play Billing — policies.google.com/privacy

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

4. Advertising & Advertising Identifiers

Where an App displays advertisements via Google AdMob, AdMob and its partners may use advertising identifiers and other technical data to serve and measure ads, subject to your device's advertising and privacy settings (e.g. "Opt out of Ads Personalization", "Limit Ad Tracking", or equivalent controls provided by Android/iOS).

5. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

Where an App offers virtual currency, one-time purchases, or subscriptions, these are processed through Google Play Billing (and, on iOS, Apple's In-App Purchase system). We do not directly collect or store your payment card details — these are handled by the respective platform.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights regarding the information collected about you, including the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data, and the right to object to or restrict certain processing. Because our Apps do not require account creation, most data we collect is anonymous or device-associated rather than tied to an identifiable individual. Where you wish to exercise a data right (for example, requesting that an advertising identifier be disassociated from analytics data), you may do so through your device's privacy settings or by contacting us using the details in Section 10.

7. Children's Privacy

Our Apps are not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required by your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that we have inadvertently collected personal information from a child, we will take steps to delete it. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us using the details in Section 10.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the information collected through our Apps against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Future Features & Updates to Data Practices

Perfect Games is an evolving studio. Future games or updates to existing games may introduce optional features such as account creation, multiplayer, leaderboards, cloud saves, friends lists, or cross-platform sync. If and when such features are introduced, this Privacy Policy will be updated beforehand to describe what additional information is collected and how it is used, and, where required by law, we will seek your consent.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, including when we add new games, new third-party SDKs, or new features. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of any App after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy. See the project README's Changelog section for how this document is maintained.
